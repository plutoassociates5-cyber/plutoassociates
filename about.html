Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

Pluto Associates was founded by Adv. Sudeep Nepal with a clear vision: to create a law firm that combines experienced legal counsel with genuine care for every client's outcome.

Together with Co-Founder Adv. Ram Sharan Ghimire, our firm has grown into a respected name in Nepal's legal landscape — covering civil, criminal, corporate, banking, family, and property law with a team of dedicated advocates and associates.

What sets us apart is our client-first philosophy. We believe every person deserves quality legal representation, regardless of the size of their case. From complex corporate transactions to personal family matters, we approach every engagement with the same level of dedication, precision, and integrity.

Today, Pluto Associates serves businesses, families, investors, and individuals across Nepal — earning trust through transparent communication, strategic thinking, and measurable results.
